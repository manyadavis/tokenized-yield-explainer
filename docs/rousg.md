rOUSG (Rebasing OUSG) is the rebasing version of Ondo's OUSG token. OUSG represents a fund of short-term US government Treasuries, with underlying assets that include BlackRock's USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Fund (BUIDL), USDC, and bank deposits. rOUSG is backed by the same underlying holdings as OUSG.

OUSG on its own is an accruing token: its price rises over time as yield accrues, while the number of tokens a holder owns stays the same.

rOUSG delivers that same yield through rebasing instead of price appreciation. Its reference price is kept fixed at approximately 1.00 dollars, and yield is passed to holders by increasing the number of rOUSG tokens in their wallet over time. Each holder's balance is multiplied by a daily rebasing factor, so the token count grows while the holder's proportional share of the fund stays the same.

The practical difference: an rOUSG holder sees a growing number of tokens at a stable one-dollar price, while an accruing token holder sees a fixed number of tokens at a rising price.

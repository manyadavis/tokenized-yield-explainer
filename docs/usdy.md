Source: Ondo Finance, USDY overview (ondo.finance/usdy)

USDY is a freely transferable yield-bearing token secured by short-term US Treasuries and bank deposits. It is designed to combine the accessibility of a stablecoin with the yield of short-term US government securities.

USDY is accessible to non-US individual and institutional investors, subject to eligibility restrictions that vary by jurisdiction. It is not offered to US persons.

USDY is an accumulating (accruing) token: yield is reflected as an increasing token price rather than as additional tokens. A holder keeps the same number of tokens over time while the value of each token rises as yield accrues. Reserves are backed by short-term US Treasuries and bank deposits, with daily third-party attestations of the collateral.
